## Why ANC Earbuds Under $100 Have Gotten Good

Two years ago, effective active noise cancellation below $100 was largely marketing fiction. The hardware required to sample, invert, and cancel ambient noise in real time was expensive. Budget ANC earbuds blocked some sound but let through enough background noise to still be distracting.

That changed. The Sony WF-C700N at $80, released in 2023, demonstrated that solid ANC was achievable at this price. Soundcore followed with the Liberty 4 NC at $80 (regularly discounted to $50–60). In 2025, multiple options at $70–100 offer ANC that was only available in $150–200 products in 2022.

The gap between $80 ANC earbuds and $250 AirPods Pro 2 is real but smaller than it used to be. If your budget caps at $100, you're no longer buying a compromised product.

## Best Under $100: Sony WF-C700N

Sony's WF-C700N brings the company's noise cancellation expertise from the flagship WF-1000XM5 down to an $80 price point. The results are impressive.

**ANC performance:** Handles low-frequency continuous noise (HVAC systems, airplane cabins, coffee shop ambience) very well. Reduces mid-frequency noise (conversations, keyboard clatter) meaningfully. Doesn't eliminate all noise — the 1000XM5 it is not — but makes library study sessions genuinely more focused.

**Fit:** The small, lightweight housing sits flush in the ear without the stem of Apple AirPods or the bulge of some competitors. Comfortable for 2–3 hour sessions without ear fatigue. Three ear tip sizes included.

**Sound:** Warm Sony signature — slightly elevated bass, smooth mids, non-fatiguing treble. Good for music, acceptable for podcasts and class calls. The built-in equalizer in the Sony app provides some customization.

**Battery:** 7.5 hours with ANC on per charge, 15 hours additional in the case. Charges via USB-C. Multipoint connection pairs with two devices simultaneously.

**Price:** Around $80, often on sale for $60–65.

## Best Budget ANC: Soundcore Liberty 4 NC

At $80 regular price (frequently $50–60 on sale), the Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC delivers ANC that has no business existing at this price. It uses 6-microphone array with adaptive ANC that adjusts to your environment automatically.

**ANC performance:** Better than the price suggests. Not as refined as the Sony — slightly more hiss in quiet environments, less effective on mid-frequency noise — but for the core use case of blocking library HVAC and cafeteria background noise, it works well.

**Extras:** LDAC high-resolution audio codec support (for Android users who care), heart rate monitor built into the earbuds, and 28 total hours battery life with the case. For $50 on sale, this value-to-feature ratio is remarkable.

**Sound:** Clear and detailed for the price. The Soundcore app equalizer is one of the better ones in this category.

**Price:** Around $80 regular, $50–60 on frequent sales.

## Best for Call Quality: Jabra Elite 4

If you take a lot of calls — remote class sessions, phone calls, Google Meet, Teams — the Jabra Elite 4 prioritizes call quality over audio performance. The 4-microphone system with wind noise suppression produces voice clarity that business users pay $150+ for in over-ear headphones.

ANC is decent but not the Elite 4's strong suit. Music playback sounds good but not exceptional. What it does best is make you sound clear on calls in noisy environments — coffee shops, transit, windy campuses.

**Price:** Around $80–90.

## Best for Android + Codec Support: EarFun Air Pro 3

For Android users who want LDAC audio codec support (enabling higher-quality streaming from Spotify, Tidal, and YouTube Music on Android), the EarFun Air Pro 3 is the pick at $80. It combines solid ANC, 45-hour total battery life, and LDAC support in one package.

Sound quality is notably better than its price suggests when streaming LDAC — approaching mid-range Sony and Samsung earbuds. ANC is competitive with the Liberty 4 NC.

**Price:** Around $80.

## How ANC Under $100 Compares to Premium Options

| Feature | Sony WF-C700N ($80) | AirPods Pro 2 ($249) |
|---|---|---|
| Low-freq ANC (HVAC) | Good | Excellent |
| Mid-freq ANC (voices) | Decent | Excellent |
| Transparency mode | Basic | Natural/transparent |
| Audio quality | Good | Very good |
| Fit stability | Good | Good |

The gap is real. If you're in the Apple ecosystem and $250 is achievable, the AirPods Pro 2's noise cancellation is better. But for the majority of college use cases — studying in libraries and cafes, commuting on campus — the Sony WF-C700N at $80 is 80% of the performance at 32% of the price.

## The Pick

Buy the Sony WF-C700N at $80. If it goes on sale below $65, it's an obvious purchase. If you're tighter on budget, the Soundcore Liberty 4 NC on sale for $50 is the better value proposition — you're not giving up as much as the price difference suggests.
